Preparation and normalization

Prepare a top eigenstate of the measured operator. With o₀ = tr(O)/dim, normalize the signal as f(t) = (⟨O(t)⟩ − o₀)/(⟨O(0)⟩ − o₀). Then f(t) = cos²θ + sin²θ cos(ωt). The target Hamiltonian closes on the same three rotation generators.
