The language of mechanisms

Realizations and their mechanisms

A realization names six components. Changing one of them either keeps the mechanism and moves it to another field, or replaces it by a different mechanism in the same material. Below, the two spins of Chapter 11 are changed one component at a time. The chain of changes reaches a magnet that keeps one of two directions of its magnetization, genetic, optical and chemical switches written by the same mechanism, oscillators locked to a drive, and conserved fields that forget as a power of time. Every mechanism, derivation and constant shown is calculated with FieldBridge; the dynamics are recalculated in the browser for the values you set.

    Path of changes

      Realizations connected by single-component changes

      Each column is a mechanism; each line changes one component, in its colour. A change along a column keeps the mechanism in another carrier; a change across columns replaces it. Selecting a realization walks the shortest path of changes to it.

      One mechanism in different fields

      Derived separately in each realization, a mechanism ends in the same canonical form and the same law constant. Each curve or point is one realization, in the units of its own derivation.

      Adding a realization

      A realization is one JSON file: the equations of a material with their source, the parameter the experiment varies, the closure and the observable. From it FieldBridge calculates the stable states, the write points and their normal forms, the writing protocols and the law of loss, and lists the realizations of the catalog that are written by the same mechanism.

      python3 -B -m fieldbridge memory new my_material --carrier torus
python3 -B -m fieldbridge memory check examples/memory/my_material.json
python3 -B -m fieldbridge memory card examples/memory/my_material.json --out-dir build/my_material

      The checks of a pull request compute the card and name the materials of other fields with the same mechanism. The Stoner–Wohlfarth particle on this page entered this way: its card places it next to the genetic toggle switch, the single-mode laser and the ring of four repressors.
