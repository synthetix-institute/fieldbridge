The language of mechanisms

Realizations and their mechanisms

Ireal = ((, ); , , ; )

FieldBridge writes every model in the language of mechanisms, as a realization of the six components in the expression above. The nesting of the expression records how the components depend on one another. An operation Ω is defined only on a carrier Ξ of states, so the pair (Ω, Ξ) forms the core of the description. The closure C states what is specified or discarded to make the equations of that core closed. The observable R and the protocol P are defined on the closed dynamics, and the parameters A, the values through which a particular material or apparatus implements the model, enter last. A prediction requires all six components, and a change of any one of them can change what is predicted.

The components separate what a system is made of from what it does. An object, such as a pair of spins, a ring of genes or a magnetic particle, fixes the carrier and the parameter values, but not the mechanism. The same two spins give a rotating or a conserved signal depending on what is measured. A spin with an anisotropy term no longer carries a Bloch rotation under closed evolution, and it keeps one of two directions when the closure makes it a damped classical moment. A ring of genes stores a state or oscillates depending on the sign of one coupling. Different objects, in turn, share one mechanism: a genetic toggle switch, a single-mode laser at threshold and a magnet written by a field along its hard axis all reduce to a supercritical pitchfork with the same canonical form, and their law constants agree within their statistical errors. What a derivation establishes, a canonical form, a law or an obstruction, therefore belongs to the mechanism and holds in every realization that reduces to it. For this reason the page is organized by mechanisms rather than by objects: a result is derived once and followed across the fields that realize it, and a mechanism found in one field becomes a candidate to test in another.

Below, the two spins of Chapter 11 are changed one component at a time. The chain of changes reaches a magnet that keeps one of two directions of its magnetization, genetic, optical and chemical switches written by the same mechanism, oscillators locked to a drive, and conserved fields that forget as a power of time. Every mechanism, derivation and constant shown is calculated with FieldBridge; the dynamics are recalculated in the browser for the values you set.

      Realizations connected by single-component changes

      Each column is a mechanism; each line changes one component, in its colour. A change along a column keeps the mechanism in another carrier; a change across columns replaces it. Selecting a realization walks the shortest path of changes to it.

      One mechanism in different fields

      Derived separately in each realization, a mechanism ends in the same canonical form and the same law constant. Each curve or point is one realization, in the units of its own derivation.

      Adding a realization

      A realization is one JSON file: the equations of a material with their source, the parameter the experiment varies, the closure and the observable. From it FieldBridge calculates the stable states, the write points and their normal forms, the writing protocols and the law of loss, and lists the realizations of the catalog that are written by the same mechanism.

      python3 -B -m fieldbridge memory new my_material --carrier torus
python3 -B -m fieldbridge memory check examples/memory/my_material.json
python3 -B -m fieldbridge memory card examples/memory/my_material.json --out-dir build/my_material

      The checks of a pull request compute the card and name the materials of other fields with the same mechanism. The Stoner–Wohlfarth particle on this page entered this way: its card places it next to the genetic toggle switch, the single-mode laser and the ring of four repressors.
