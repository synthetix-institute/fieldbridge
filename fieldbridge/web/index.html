The six components
Selected mechanism

FieldBridge writes a model as a realization of six components. An operation Ω acts on a carrier Ξ of states, and this pair is the core. The closure C makes its equations closed, R is what is measured, P how the system is prepared and driven, and A the parameter values of a particular material or apparatus.

A mechanism is what remains when the carrier and the parameter values are left open: a canonical form and its laws. A magnet, a genetic switch and a laser differ as objects but keep one of two states by the same mechanism, so its law is derived once and holds in each. The page therefore starts from mechanisms, not from objects.

FieldBridge is developed within the Synthetix Institute. The code, the specification of every realization shown here and the tutorial are in the FieldBridge repository; MorphWiki, which organizes the explanations, equations and source evidence of a field, and the institute's other public repositories are on GitHub.

Each card is a mechanism. A line is a change of one component, marked with its symbol, that takes a realization of one mechanism to a realization of the other. The filled card is the selected mechanism, and the outlined cards are one change away. Selecting a card reaches that mechanism by the shortest chain of changes.

Chain of changes

    Changing one component of a mechanism

    1. Start. The expression below is the pitchfork normal form, the mechanism of every symmetric write, written without any field.
    2. Change one component. Each coloured button under Ω, Ξ, C, R, P or A is a verified change of that component, and it names the mechanism it leads to. Sliders change the values of A within the realization.
    3. Compare. The mechanism, its equations and its dynamics are recalculated. A change of Ξ keeps the mechanism and places it in a field; the other components can replace it by another mechanism.

      Each row is one mechanism, and its realizations are grouped by field. Selecting a field marks the mechanisms it contains; selecting a realization shows its source and opens it in the instrument.

      Sources of all realizations

      Adding a realization

      A realization is one JSON file: the equations of a material with their source, the parameter the experiment varies, the closure and the observable. From it FieldBridge calculates the stable states, the write points and their normal forms, the writing protocols and the law of loss, and lists the realizations of the catalog that are written by the same mechanism.

      python3 -B -m fieldbridge memory new my_material --carrier torus
python3 -B -m fieldbridge memory check examples/memory/my_material.json
python3 -B -m fieldbridge memory card examples/memory/my_material.json --out-dir build/my_material

      The checks of a pull request compute the card and name the materials of other fields with the same mechanism. The Stoner–Wohlfarth particle on this page entered this way: its card places it next to the genetic toggle switch, the single-mode laser and the ring of four repressors.
