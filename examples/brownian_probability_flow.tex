A thermally buffeted state follows
\[
  d x=-\nabla U(x)\,dt+\sqrt{2T}\,dW,
\]
with probability flow
\[
  \partial_t p=\nabla\!\cdot(p\nabla U)+T\nabla^2p.
\]
Probability is normalized, the boundary has zero probability current, and the
stationary state is proportional to \(\exp[-U/T]\). The measurable outputs are
the evolving density, relaxation time and free energy. Reversing the drift or
removing the noise provides clause-breaking controls.
